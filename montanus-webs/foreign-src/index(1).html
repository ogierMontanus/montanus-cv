About the site

Deutsch - dansk

This website helps you to keep track of my professional activity. In the course of my career as a historian, I have crossed some boundaries in both the physical and the professional sense of the term. I received my bachelor's degree in Copenhagen, Denmark and then my master's in Erfurt, Germany. After completing the Ph.D.-programme in Florence, Italy in 2008, I then worked in the museum world. I currently work in the field of scholarly digital editions (see the cv for more details).

I publish in English, German and Danish. Excerpts from articles and chapters are available, when copyright allows it. Click on links to learn about my professional interests. Recent articles are listed first.

English rules the web - including this site. If you wish to read it in German or Danish, you have to choose your language anew in every section. I apologise for the inconvenience.
