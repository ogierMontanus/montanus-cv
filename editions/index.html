Caspar Heinrich Marx (1600–1635): Diarium Actorum a tempore caesi Exercitus Caesariani

Diary of the acts following the defeat of the Imperial army on September 17/7, in the year 1631

With Hans Medick, Norbert Winnige, Thomas Rokahr, Bernd Warlich.

This official journal was kept by a local priest in the Thirty Years War during the first Swedish occupation of Erfurt (1631–1635). Despite its Latin title, the journal is mainly written in German. Published online both in facsimile and in a modernised transcript with ample notes and an introduction. One of five online ego-documents from Central Germany during the Thirty Years War.

http://www.mdsz.thulb.uni-jena.de/marx/quelle.php
